Frequently asked questions

Questions about ExpenseFlow?

Everything you need to know about tracking expenses and managing your records.

What is ExpenseFlow?

ExpenseFlow is a personal expense tracker. You sign in, record each expense with a date, category, and amount, and review your spending by day, by month, and by category.

How do I add a new expense?

Sign in, open Add Expense, enter the amount, choose the date and category, add an optional description, and select Save Expense.

Can I view my expenses by day or month?

Yes. The History page groups your expenses by month or by day, and you can filter by category or search descriptions.

What expense categories are available?

Food & Dining, Transport, Shopping, Bills & Utilities, Entertainment, and Health.

Can I see charts of my spending?

Yes. The Dashboard shows spending by week, month, or year, a category breakdown, and recent transactions. The Insights page adds monthly progress and a 30-day history.

Do I need an account to manage my expenses?

Yes. Each account keeps its own expenses, so you only see your own records.
